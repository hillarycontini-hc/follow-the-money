### Eunisses Hernandez — CD1, 2022 — **WON**
- **Result source:** https://abc7.com/eunisses-hernandez-gil-cedillo-los-angeles-city-council-district-1/11981005 — Hernandez won outright in the June 7, 2022 primary against incumbent Gil Cedillo and Elaine Alaniz with "more than 53%" of the vote to Cedillo's 46% (as of Monday morning post-election reporting).

### Gil Cedillo — CD1, 2022 — **LOST**
- **Result source:** Same as above — Cedillo received ~46% to Hernandez's ~53%+ in the June 7, 2022 primary, losing outright without advancing to a runoff. https://abc7.com/eunisses-hernandez-gil-cedillo-los-angeles-city-council-district-1/11981005
